# Gamepad Keyboard

## 1.1.0

- Boxes of numbers - a price, a quantity, an amount of gold - open on a page of just the number row and
  Enter. Letters and Space don't go in.
- The mail's money (gold, silver, copper) and the Auction House's sell form (quantity and price) work with
  the keyboard. Enter in the mail's gold moves on to silver, then copper, and the keyboard follows.

## 1.0.0

First release: an on-screen keyboard for World of Warcraft: Forever's controller UI.

- Opens when you go to a text box with the controller (A on it, or Chat on the main menu dial), as part of
  the game's controller UI. A real keyboard or the mouse never brings it up.
- D-pad between keys, A types, hold A for Enter, X deletes, Y is Space, LB / RB move the cursor, B goes
  back.
- Chat: Enter sends and closes the chat. The chat key steps through Say, Party, Raid, Instance, Guild, the
  whisper you started and a reply to your last whisperer. Slash commands and numbered channels work, and
  shift-clicked item links go into the line. A line closed without sending waits for next time.
- Mail: To:, Subject: and the letter. Names come up as you type in To:. In the letter, Enter starts a new
  line.
- Other addons' text boxes work too. The game's own (the bag search, the Options search, confirmation
  pop-ups) are left to a real keyboard: typing into them from an addon gets the addon blocked.
- Options: open with the controller, QWERTY or Dvorak, close after Enter, a capital to start a chat line,
  name suggestions, hold time for Enter, and size. Place it in Edit Mode.
