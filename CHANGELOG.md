# Gamepad Keyboard

## 1.2.0

- The Auction House's search box works with the keyboard. Enter (or holding A) presses its Search button,
  the same as pressing it yourself.
- The chat key opens a list of every chat - Say, Party, Raid, Instance, Guild, Officer, Yell, then
  whispers - with the cursor on the one you're in: D-pad up / down, A picks, B goes back. Every chat is
  there whether you're in a group or guild or not; one you aren't in gets the game's own message.
- The line shows its chat in front of it - "Guild:", "To Bob:" - in the chat's colour.
- More of the game's text boxes work with the keyboard: the searches in Mounts, Pets, Appearances, the
  transmogrifier, Professions (and its crafting orders) and the AddOns list; Who; the Guild and Community
  Finder; a crafting order's recipient and tip; a currency transfer's amount; the alarm's message; a guild
  charter's name; the Black Market's bid; an Auction House item's bid. Where the game's own Enter would
  get the addon blocked (Who, the Finders, crafting orders' search, the guild charter), Enter presses the
  window's button instead - Search, Purchase - as you would.
- The Auction House's buy quantity (a stack) says why it doesn't open, and what to do instead: A on a row
  of the price list buys up to it.

## 1.1.0

- Boxes of numbers - a price, a quantity, an amount of gold - open on a numpad (7 8 9 / 4 5 6 / 1 2 3 /
  Del 0 Enter). Letters and Space don't go in.
- The mail's money (gold, silver, copper) and the Auction House's sell form (quantity and price) work with
  the keyboard. Enter in the mail's gold moves on to silver, then copper, and the keyboard follows.
- A link already in the chat box (shift-clicked with the chat box open, or put there by a "link in chat")
  shows in the keyboard's preview. The chat key switches its chat too - Say, Guild, Party... - and Enter
  takes you back to the chat box, where A sends it.

## 1.0.0

First release: an on-screen keyboard for World of Warcraft: Forever's controller UI.

- Opens when you go to a text box with the controller (A on it, or Chat on the main menu dial), as part of
  the game's controller UI. A real keyboard or the mouse never brings it up.
- D-pad between keys, A types, hold A for Enter, X deletes, Y is Space, LB / RB move the cursor, B goes
  back.
- Chat: Enter sends and closes the chat. The chat key steps through Say, Party, Raid, Instance, Guild, the
  whisper you started and a reply to your last whisperer. Slash commands and numbered channels work, and
  shift-clicked item links go into the line. A line closed without sending waits for next time.
- Mail: To:, Subject: and the letter. Names come up as you type in To:. In the letter, Enter starts a new
  line.
- Other addons' text boxes work too. The game's own (the bag search, the Options search, confirmation
  pop-ups) are left to a real keyboard: typing into them from an addon gets the addon blocked.
- Options: open with the controller, QWERTY or Dvorak, close after Enter, a capital to start a chat line,
  name suggestions, hold time for Enter, and size. Place it in Edit Mode.
